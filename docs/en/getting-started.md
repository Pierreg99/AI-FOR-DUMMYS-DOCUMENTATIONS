# Quick start

Find your starting point in AI and try a small example in 30 minutes.

## 1. Get oriented: five minutes

Read [AI fundamentals](01-ai-fundamentals.md). Separate the model, application, and autonomy. Choose a concrete everyday task, such as searching an FAQ or summarizing instructions.

## 2. Understand: ten minutes

Read [Language models](04-llms.md) and [RAG](06-rag.md). Define a good answer, a failure case, and a situation where the system should ask a follow-up question.

## 3. Try it: ten minutes

The [practice projects](projects.md) include a local search example without API keys. You need Python 3.10 or later. It finds documents through shared words and demonstrates why retrieval and answer quality are different things.

## 4. Check it: five minutes

Record one successful result and one failure. Explain the difference through data, retrieval, or generation. Mark a chapter complete only when you can answer its exercise.

## Your next learning path

- Foundations: chapters 1–6 for concepts and connections.
- Build agents: chapters 7–9, 14–15, 17, 24, 27, 29–30.
- Operate systems: chapters 19–23, 25–26, 31–32, 34–36.

Reading times are estimates at approximately 180 words per minute. Allow extra time for exercises. Website progress and bookmarks are stored only in your browser; there is no account or cross-device synchronization.
